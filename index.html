Family-owned student housing

An 1889 house, a brand-new building, one address.

The Luttrell joins the Captain James F. and Cecilia M. Luttrell House, a City of Berkeley designated historic resource built in 1889, with a new 13-unit building behind it. Pick a private or shared bedroom in a fully furnished home with a full kitchen, keyless entry and an elevator. UC Berkeley, Telegraph Avenue and everything in between are a few minutes away on foot.
