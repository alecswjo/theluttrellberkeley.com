Neighborhood

Southside Berkeley, on foot.

2328 Channing Way sits on the block between Ellsworth and Dana Streets, a short walk to UC Berkeley, Telegraph Avenue and downtown.

Walking times

  • 5min walkTelegraph AvenueBookstores, record shops, cafés and late-night food, at the corner of Channing Way.
  • 8min walkSproul PlazaThe main southern entrance to the UC Berkeley campus.
  • 9min walkSather GateThe landmark 1910 gateway into the heart of campus.
  • 12min walkCaffè StradaA long-time student café at College Avenue and Bancroft Way.
  • 13min walkDoe LibraryThe university's main library, just past Sather Gate.
  • 14min walkDowntown Berkeley BARTTrains to San Francisco, Oakland and the rest of the Bay Area.
  • 21min walkBerkeley BowlThe city's beloved grocery store, on Oregon Street at Shattuck Avenue.
  • 21min walkTrader Joe'sGroceries on University Avenue at Martin Luther King Jr. Way.

Walking times from 2328 Channing Way are estimates based on OpenStreetMap walking routes, rounded up. Your pace may vary.
