Amenities

Everything you need, nothing you don't.

Move in with a suitcase. Bedrooms come furnished, kitchens come complete, and the building handles the rest.

Your Apartment

  • Fully furnished bedroomsPrivate rooms have a full bed, desk, chair, two 2-drawer chests and a wardrobe. Shared rooms have two Twin XL beds, two desks and chairs, and two wardrobes.
  • Private or shared roomsEvery plan has private bedrooms. Layouts 1 and 2 and the Luttrell House also have a room for two.
  • Full kitchenEvery apartment has its own full kitchen.
  • Keyless unit entryNo keys to carry or lose. Your apartment door opens without one.
  • One to three bathroomsLayouts 1, 2 and 3 have one bathroom, Layout 4 has two, and the Luttrell House has three.

Your Building

  • Keyless, secure building entryKeyless access at the building door, with security for residents.
  • ElevatorAn elevator serves every floor of the new building.
  • On-site laundryWashers and dryers in the building.
  • Bike roomSecure indoor storage for your bike.
  • A historic landmark next doorThe 1889 Luttrell House, a City of Berkeley designated historic resource, sits at the front of the property.
  • Walk to campusAbout an 8-minute walk to Sproul Plaza and 5 minutes to Telegraph Avenue.
